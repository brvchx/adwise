Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what AdWise — Ad Blocker & VPN (the "App") collects, why, and what it does not collect. The App is published by ASKOR SIA ("we", "us"), a company registered in Latvia, European Union.

Contact for any privacy question: adblockdev@outlook.com.

1. The short version

  • We do not require an account. You never give us your name, email or phone number to use the App.
  • We do not log your browsing history, the sites you visit or the contents of your traffic.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone.
  • Blocking happens on your device. Filter lists are stored locally.
  • We do collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and — only with your permission — advertising identifiers used to measure where installs come from.

2. What the App collects

The App does not ask for and does not collect your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, health data, precise location or files.

3. Tracking and the IDFA

On first launch iOS shows a system dialog asking whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the App does not access your advertising identifier and no tracking takes place. Everything in the App keeps working exactly the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Ad and content blocking

Blocking runs entirely on your device, using the content blocking mechanism built into iOS. Filter lists are downloaded from public sources, stored locally and applied by Safari itself. We never see which pages you open, and the App does not send your browsing history anywhere. The counter of blocked requests is stored on your device only.

5. VPN

When you connect to the VPN, your traffic is encrypted and routed through a server we operate. Specifically:

  • We do not keep connection logs, timestamps, bandwidth records or the addresses you visit through the tunnel.
  • We do not inspect, decrypt, store or modify the contents of your traffic.
  • We do not sell, share or disclose any VPN usage data to advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.
  • Servers assign you a temporary internal address from a shared pool. It is not tied to your identity and is reused by other users.
  • Our servers are hosted with DigitalOcean in Germany, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, the United States and Singapore.

We may process aggregate, non-identifying technical metrics such as total server load, in order to keep the service running. These metrics cannot be linked to an individual user.

6. Subscriptions and payments

Paid features are sold as auto-renewable subscriptions through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple — we never see or receive your card details. We receive from Apple, through our subscription provider Apphud, only an anonymous receipt confirming whether a subscription is active, its type and its expiry date. This is used solely to unlock paid features on your devices and to restore purchases.

A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in the Settings app under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

7. Third-party services

The services listed below process data on our behalf. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • AppsFlyer (install attribution) — appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy
  • Apphud (subscription management) — apphud.com/privacy
  • Branch (deep links and install attribution) — branch.io/policies
  • Apple (payments, App Store) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • DigitalOcean (VPN server hosting) — digitalocean.com/legal/privacy-policy

8. Data retention

Anonymous analytics and crash data are kept by the services above for as long as their own retention policies allow, typically up to 14 months, and then deleted automatically. Subscription status is kept for as long as the subscription is active and for the period required by tax and accounting law. VPN connection data is not retained at all, because it is not recorded in the first place.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to request access to your data, its correction or deletion, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of your data. Since we do not hold accounts, the fastest way to exercise these rights is to write to adblockdev@outlook.com — include your device model and approximate install date so that we can locate the relevant records. We answer within 30 days.

You may also withdraw tracking consent at any time in iOS settings, and you may delete all locally stored data by removing the App from your device.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are covered by the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission or by equivalent safeguards offered by those providers.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify users inside the App. The current version is always available at this address.
